# Your first payment

Nano (XNO) is digital money that moves in under a second with no fees. Merchants ask for it because a payment of a few cents costs the buyer nothing extra and needs no account, card, or sign-up with the merchant.

Paying takes three steps: get a wallet, put a little Nano in it, then scan the merchant's QR code.

## 1. Get a wallet

A wallet is an app that holds your Nano. Install one on your phone:

- <a href="https://cakewallet.com/" target="_blank">Cake Wallet</a> (iOS, Android, desktop) — open source and actively maintained, holds Nano alongside other currencies.
- <a href="https://nautilus.io/" target="_blank">Nautilus</a> (iOS, Android, macOS) — open source, built only for Nano.

When the wallet shows you a secret phrase, write it down and keep it somewhere safe. Anyone with that phrase controls your Nano, and without it a lost phone means lost funds. For amounts you plan to keep, see [Account Setup](/getting-started-users/storing/setup).

<small>_More wallets are listed on the <a href="https://hub.nano.org/wallets" target="_blank">Nano Hub</a>. None are endorsed by nano.community; please do your own research._</small>

## 2. Get a little Nano

Few card services sell Nano directly, and which ones work depends on your country, so buy a few dollars' worth once to cover several payments rather than one.

- **From an exchange:** <a href="https://www.kraken.com/prices/nano" target="_blank">Kraken</a> trades Nano against US dollars and euros. Buy there, then withdraw to your wallet's address.
- **By swapping another coin:** if you already hold Bitcoin, Litecoin, or another common coin, or can buy one with a card, swap it for Nano on <a href="https://nanswap.com/" target="_blank">Nanswap</a> and have it sent straight to your wallet's address.
- **To try it for free:** a faucet such as <a href="https://nanodrop.io/" target="_blank">NanoDrop</a> gives out tiny amounts. That is enough to see how sending works, though usually not enough to pay for something.

See [Acquiring](/getting-started-users/acquiring) for more exchanges and services by country.

## 3. Pay

1. On the merchant's page, choose what you are buying. It shows a QR code.
2. In your wallet, tap **Send** and scan the code. The address and the exact amount fill in for you.
3. Check the amount and confirm.

The payment settles in about a second, and the merchant's page updates on its own. Send the exact amount shown: many merchants give each purchase its own address and match the payment to it.
